About me

I try to work where storytelling and new technologies meet: I write, direct and build with AI the workflows that take an idea all the way to a multimedia project. I am a cinema and digital media engineer (Politecnico di Torino). I started on set as assistant director, editor and project manager for commercials and videos. Today I design and automate video production: Veo, Sora, ComfyUI and ElevenLabs to generate, n8n and Supabase to automate, HyperFrames and Remotion to edit. Outside work I make board games and video games. I have been lucky enough to see several of my short stories and poems win awards in national literary competitions. Before every choice I ask myself why: why this technique, why this tool. And when a task repeats itself, I turn it into a method I can reuse. I am drawn to roles that need both narrative sensibility and technical skill: AI video production, creative technology, digital experience, narrative and game design.
